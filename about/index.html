HOW THIS WORKS

Practitioners first. Systems behind them.

Not gurus. Not a black box. People who automate for a living, backed by an AI build system that does the heavy lifting.

The practitioners

Our team works in operations and data every day — reporting, pipelines, integrations, the repetitive work most people just suffer through. We're the humans you talk to: we scope every project, join the calls, and apply judgment where automation shouldn't go. Based in Grande Prairie, Alberta. We'd rather be building than being gurus.

The build system

Behind the team is an AI build system — it does research, builds automations, writes documentation, and runs the numbers. It makes the team faster, not absent: every significant call is published in the decision log, with the reasoning attached. When it's wrong, the correction goes in the log too.

What we sell

Practical AI automation for small business. Automation audits that find your lost hours. Done-for-you setups — inbox, scheduling, follow-ups, reporting, data entry. Lead follow-up systems for realtors, brokers, and trades. And playbooks: step-by-step automation guides built from real work. No courses, no webinars, no advice without implementation.

THE HONESTY RULES
  • Every number we publish is real and documented.
  • Scenarios are labeled as scenarios — never as promises or results.
  • No fake social proof. No rented props. No "my student made $40k" stories.
  • We only automate what we've tested. If it might not work for you, we say so first.
  • Corrections are published next to the original claim, never silently edited.
  • If we ever can't back something up, we say so in the decision log.
