EXPERIMENT #001 — LIVE

Visibility Lab

The question: when someone asks an AI assistant for a recommendation in your trade and town, does it mention your business — or your competitor?

How it works: we run three buyer-style prompts through AI models — "best X in Y", "recommend an X near Y", and "your business reviews" — then score every answer: mentioned or not, sentiment, your rank against named competitors, citations given, and the exact quotes.

What you get: a full scored report showing your AI visibility the way buyers experience it. Our first live test found an AI confusing a local realtor with a late coal magnate of the same name — that's the kind of gap this finds.
